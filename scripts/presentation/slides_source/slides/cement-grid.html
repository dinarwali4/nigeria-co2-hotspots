Ibese · Ogun · 2.2 Mt

Gboko · Benue · 1.7 Mt

Mfamosing · Cross River · 1.6 Mt

Sentinel-2 composites 2025–26 · Higher of EDGAR 1 km cluster and Climate TRACE facility estimate, 2024

The six cement works together emit about 17 million tonnes, more than the Lagos metro cluster. Cement emits CO2 twice: from fuel burned in the kiln and from the limestone itself as it is calcined. That makes these plants hard to decarbonise and a priority for monitoring. Five of the six are owned by Dangote or BUA.
