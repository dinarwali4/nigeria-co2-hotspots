Emissions: Climate TRACE 2024. Not yet visible in EDGAR's 1 km grid · Imagery: Landsat 7 SR, Sentinel-2 composites, 7 km frames

The clearest change in the whole study. In 2000 to 2002 the Lekki site was coastal bush. By 2016 and 2017 it was a sand-filled platform. By 2025 and 2026 it is a complete 650,000 barrel per day refinery with tank farms. Climate TRACE puts its 2024 emissions at 2.65 million tonnes, its first operating year. EDGAR's 1 km grid does not include it yet, so inventory-only maps miss it.
