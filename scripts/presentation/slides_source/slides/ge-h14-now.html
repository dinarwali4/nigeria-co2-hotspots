H14 · Persistent fire cluster, Ogba/Egbema area

Largest MODIS persistent-fire cluster 2024–25: 19 km², fire on up to 229 of 730 days

Now · 30 Dec 2022

Google Earth Pro · annotated by Dinar Wali

The centre of the largest persistent-fire cluster, in December 2022. Labelled: the most persistent fire pixel, accurate to about half a kilometre, and a river channel. No flare stack is visible in this scene; the January 2014 scene also shows only forest. The fire record is real, but its source cannot be seen in Google Earth's imagery, so this site needs higher-resolution or night-time imagery.
