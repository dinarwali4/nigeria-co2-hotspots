The coast and the cement belt

Lagos and the Port Harcourt–Bonny corridor hold the largest clusters.

Cement works form a second belt inland, from Ogun through Kogi and Benue to Cross River.

Orange dots are gas flares, all in the Niger Delta.

The map shows EDGAR's 1 kilometre fossil-CO2 grid for 2024 on a log scale. The 16 circles are the candidate hotspots. Two coastal clusters dominate, Lagos and the Port Harcourt to Bonny corridor. Inland, a belt of cement works stands out. The orange dots are 1 km pixels where the MODIS satellite saw fire on at least 60 of 730 days in 2024 and 2025, which identifies gas flares; they are all in the Niger Delta.
