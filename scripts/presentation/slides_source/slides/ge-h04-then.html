H04 · Bonny Island – Nigeria LNG

EDGAR cluster 5.4 Mt CO₂/yr in 100 km² · LNG trains, power plant and export terminal

Then · 13 Jan 2014

Google Earth Pro · annotated by Dinar Wali

Bonny Island in January 2014, the clearest scene available. Labelled: the LNG storage tanks, the LNG process trains where gas is treated and liquefied, the loading jetties, and the oil export terminal tanks. The process trains are where most of the CO2 comes from: CO2 stripped from the feed gas plus fuel burned on site.
