Limits and next steps

What the data cannot say

CO₂ is not measured directly. No satellite yet resolves one plant's plume over Nigeria; each site is an inventory figure checked with NO₂.

Flare counts are not volumes. MODIS overpass times changed after 2020.

Land and fire CO₂ are excluded. Forest and savanna fires add roughly 100 Mt.

What comes next

Monthly NO₂ watch on the 15 sites, starting with the cement belt and the Dangote refinery.

High-resolution tasking over the 23 flare sites to map individual flare stacks.

Direct CO₂ plumes from the Copernicus CO2M mission once it is in orbit.

Three limits. First, CO2 itself is not measured: no satellite yet resolves a single plant's plume over Nigeria, so each hotspot is an inventory figure confirmed with NO2. Second, the change in flare detections is not a change in flared volume. Third, land-use and fire CO2, which Climate TRACE puts near 100 million tonnes, is outside this fossil-fuel analysis. Next steps: a monthly NO2 watch on the verified sites, high-resolution tasking over the flare sites, and direct plume measurement once the Copernicus CO2M mission is operating.
