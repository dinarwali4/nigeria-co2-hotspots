The inventory puts flares in the wrong place

EDGAR spreads oil-and-gas emissions over coarse 0.1° blocks (blue squares).

The flares MODIS sees burning for months (orange) mostly sit outside them.

23 flare sites in 2024–25, all between 4.3° and 6.1° N.

MODIS fire on ≥60 of 730 days. Counts are not volumes.

Flaring is Nigeria's best-known CO2 source, but the inventory places it badly. EDGAR distributes oil and gas emissions over coarse 0.1 degree blocks, the blue squares. The orange dots are 1 km pixels where MODIS saw fire on at least 60 of 730 days, which only a flare does. They mostly sit outside EDGAR's blocks. There are 23 such sites in 2024 and 2025. One caution: more detections than in 2014 and 2015 does not mean more flaring. MODIS overpass times changed, and World Bank figures show Nigerian flared volumes falling.
