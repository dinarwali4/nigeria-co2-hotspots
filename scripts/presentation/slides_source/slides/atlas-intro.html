This section brings in the Google Earth Pro atlas. For each hotspot there is a historical view, usually around 2013, and the latest view, with every visible feature labelled: kiln lines, quarries, tank farms, process units, flare pits and well pads. Each image carries a scale bar, a north arrow and the imagery provider. The atlas pages follow the story slide for the same site, so the deck moves from the inventory number to what is actually on the ground.
