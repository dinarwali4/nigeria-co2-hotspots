National NO₂ background rose 16.5% over 2019–2025 · Landsat 7 SR and Sentinel-2 · Emissions: EDGAR / Climate TRACE 2024

Cement is the fastest-growing source. None of these plants existed in the 2000 to 2002 Landsat imagery. Obajana, now Africa's largest cement plant, emits about 6.1 million tonnes. Okpotu's NO2 rose 45 percent between 2019 and 2025 and Mfamosing's 36 percent, against a national background rise of 16.5 percent, so they are growing faster than the country. Okpotu does not appear in EDGAR's grid at all.
