Gas-fired power is the third group. Azura-Edo, near Benin City, was farmland in 2000 to 2002 and is now a 461 megawatt plant emitting about 2.1 million tonnes. Ughelli, in Delta State, emits about 1.8 million tonnes, and persistent gas flares from the surrounding oil fields sit just south-east of it.
