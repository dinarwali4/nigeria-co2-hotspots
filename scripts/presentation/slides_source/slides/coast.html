Sentinel-2 cloud-masked composites, 2025–26 · Emissions: EDGAR 2025 1 km clusters, 2024

The coast carries the largest emissions. Lagos is a 962 square kilometre cluster of road transport, the Egbin gas plant and industry: 12.2 million tonnes. Port Harcourt, Onne and Eleme combine two fertiliser complexes, the refineries and the Afam power stations: 9.3 million tonnes, and the highest NO2 of any site, 2.3 times the national median. Bonny Island's LNG trains emit 5.4 million tonnes from only 100 square kilometres.
