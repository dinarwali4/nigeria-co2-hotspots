Nigeria CO₂ Hotspots

Where the country's fossil carbon comes from, located with emission inventories, checked from orbit and in Google Earth, 2000 to 2026.

Prepared by Dinar Wali

Dangote refinery, Lekki · 2025–26

Opening. This deck locates Nigeria's fossil-CO2 hotspots using the EDGAR 2025 emission grid and Climate TRACE facility estimates, then checks each one with satellite data: Sentinel-5P NO2, MODIS gas-flare persistence, Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery, and Google's AlphaEarth embeddings. The background image is the Dangote refinery at Lekki, the newest large emitter in the country.
