Method

Find it in the inventories, prove it from orbit

1 · Locate

Emission grids

EDGAR 1 km CO₂ for 2024, clustered above 3,300 t/km²

2 · Attribute

Facilities

Climate TRACE estimates for 259 plants, ports and fields

3 · Flares

Fire that never stops

MODIS pixels burning on 60+ of 730 days

4 · Verify

From orbit

Sentinel-5P NO₂, Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery, AlphaEarth

Four steps. First, locate clusters in EDGAR's 1 km CO2 grid, using the threshold above which pixels hold half of national emissions. Second, attribute them with Climate TRACE facility estimates. Third, map gas flares directly: savanna fires last days, so a pixel that burns for months is a flare. Fourth, verify every site from orbit: NO2 from Sentinel-5P, true-colour imagery from 2000 to 2026, and Google's AlphaEarth embeddings, which is the method in the original Earth Engine script.
