Three numbers frame the problem. Nigeria emitted about 137.6 million tonnes of fossil CO2 in 2025, 58 percent more than in 2005. And the emissions are extremely concentrated: half of the total comes from just 4,425 square kilometres, about 0.57 percent of the country. That concentration is what makes a hotspot approach worthwhile.
