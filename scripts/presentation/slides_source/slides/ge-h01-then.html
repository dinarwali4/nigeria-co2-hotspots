H01 · Egbin power station, Ikorodu (Lagos)

Lagos metro cluster 12.2 Mt CO₂/yr (EDGAR 2024) · Egbin 2.06 Mt (Climate TRACE) · 1,320 MW gas-fired

Then · 2 May 2013

Google Earth Pro · annotated by Dinar Wali

Egbin power station on the Lagos Lagoon shore in May 2013. Labelled: the power station, operating since the 1980s, and its fuel and gas storage tanks. Egbin is a long-standing source: the plant was already here well before the 2005 baseline, and Climate TRACE puts it at 2.06 million tonnes of CO2 in 2024.
