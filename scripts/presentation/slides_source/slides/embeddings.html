GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL · 500 samples, wekaKMeans, as in the linked script · Change = dot product of 2017 and 2024 vectors

The linked Earth Engine script clusters Google's AlphaEarth satellite embeddings with k-means. Embeddings describe what a surface looks like, not what it emits, so I used them to characterise and date each hotspot. In the middle panel, eight clusters separate the tank farm, the process units, bare sand and vegetation. On the right, the dot product between the 2017 and 2024 embedding vectors shows where the surface changed: 58 percent of the area within 2 km of the refinery.
